# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** June 14, 2026

This privacy policy describes how Tethys ("we", "our", or "us") handles your information when you use our mobile application.

## 1. Information We Collect

**We do not collect any personal information.**

The App does not:

- Collect, store, or transmit your name, email address, phone number, or any other personal identifiers.
- Access your location, contacts, calendar, photos, or files.
- Use cameras, microphones, or any device sensors beyond what is required to render the watch face and display the current time.
- Request network permissions or internet access.
- Use analytics services, crash reporting tools, or third-party SDKs that collect data.
- Include advertisements or tracking of any kind.

## 2. How We Use Information

Since we collect no information, no information is used, processed, or shared.

## 3. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with any third-party services, analytics frameworks, or advertising networks. No data is sent to external servers.

## 4. Data Retention & Deletion

As no personal data is collected, there is nothing to retain or delete.

## 5. Children's Privacy

The App does not collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.
